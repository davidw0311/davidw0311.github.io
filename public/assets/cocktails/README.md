# Cocktail photography

Official Jigger & Pony BLOOM cocktail photographs, credited in the app library.
Source checked 18 November 2026: https://www.jiggerandpony.com/bloom-menu

All photographs except the four homepage images listed below were extracted
from the official linked BLOOM PDF:
https://cdnc.heyzine.com/files/uploaded/v3/790674ab95e832b771ee97c18f9cd833bc92bd3b.pdf

Yuzu Whisky Sour, Penicillin, Wasabi Highball and Negroni were downloaded from
the official homepage's Squarespace image CDN. Copyright remains with the
respective photographers / Jigger & Pony; this app makes no ownership claim.

The collection covers all 21 alcoholic cocktails in BLOOM. It excludes the three
Free Spirited drinks, punch bowls, and the spirit list.

Manhattan and Champagne Ramos Gin Fizz share the menu’s photograph of both drinks.
Singa Sling is pictured beside Yuzu Whisky Sour in the original menu photograph.
Measurements and methods are independent home adaptations, labelled in the UI.
