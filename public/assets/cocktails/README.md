# Cocktail photography

Official Jigger & Pony BLOOM cocktail photographs, credited in the app library.
Source checked 18 November 2026: https://www.jiggerandpony.com/bloom-menu

All photographs except the four homepage images listed below were extracted
from the official linked BLOOM PDF:
https://cdnc.heyzine.com/files/uploaded/v3/790674ab95e832b771ee97c18f9cd833bc92bd3b.pdf

Yuzu Whisky Sour, Penicillin, Wasabi Highball and Negroni were downloaded from
the official homepage's Squarespace image CDN. Copyright remains with the
respective photographers / Jigger & Pony; this app makes no ownership claim.

The collection covers all 21 alcoholic cocktails in BLOOM. It excludes the three
Free Spirited drinks, punch bowls, and the spirit list.

Manhattan and Champagne Ramos Gin Fizz share the menu’s photograph of both drinks.
Singa Sling is pictured beside Yuzu Whisky Sour in the original menu photograph.
Measurements and methods are independent home adaptations, labelled in the UI.

## World classics

Twelve additional classic recipes and photographs are referenced from the
International Bartenders Association (IBA), checked 18 November 2026. Each card
links directly to its recipe page at `https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/<id>/`.
The classic images are the `.webp` files named mojito, margarita, daiquiri,
cosmopolitan, mai-tai, paloma, moscow-mule, spritz, french-75, pina-colada,
whiskey-sour, and dry-martini. They were downloaded from the corresponding
recipe pages on iba-world.com; copyright remains with the respective owners.
Recipe instructions are paraphrased for the card format. The Spritz card uses
the display name Aperol Spritz, matching the Aperol specification on IBA’s page.

## Mama Diam — October 2026

Twelve signatures are drawn from the October 2026 menu linked by the official
site, https://www.mamadiamsg.com/menu, checked 18 November 2026:
https://www.mamadiamsg.com/s/MMD_Oct2026Menu_Digital.pdf

The twelve corresponding PNGs are the original menu illustrations extracted
from pages 11–13. They illustrate the memories behind each drink and are not
photographs of the finished cocktails. Copyright remains with Mama Diam and
its artists. Each card credits the menu and identifies the recipe as a home
adaptation: measurements, preparation methods and shortcuts are our own,
not published recipes from the bar.
