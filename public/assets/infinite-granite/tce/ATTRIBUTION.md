# TCE Stone photography and catalogue

Product names, specifications, and slab/detail photographs are from TCE Trading (Canada) Inc. / TCE Stone: https://tcestone.com/products/. Source pages and image downloads were audited September 7, 2026. Copyright remains with the supplier; these photographs are not independently generated or represented as public domain.

The five listing pages contain 97 unique codes (20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 17), despite the catalogue heading saying 94 colours. All 97 are included: 77 full-slab photographs and 20 detail photographs. Twenty-one products were explicitly marked clearance/discontinued with limited stock. The supplier describes this collection as quartz.

`catalogue.json` preserves every product URL, original photograph URL, source/optimized image checksums, finish, availability, published slab dimensions and preview dimensions. `{code}.webp` is the optimized original photograph; `{code}-thumb.webp` is its cropped catalogue thumbnail. Original downloads and retrieved HTML are not included in this website.

Full-slab textures use published physical dimensions. Detail-only photographs use an estimated preview width of 48 inches and retain their aspect ratio; the editor labels this approximate scale. Colours, lighting, cropping and vein positions in a rendering are illustrative. Confirm a physical sample and available stock with the supplier.

The official original images are identical for product pairs 2013 Crystal Frost / 2019 Crystal White, and 4001 Pure White / 9001 Pure White. Both official codes and their source photographs are retained.

The source product pages provide labeled image downloads and a supplier copyright notice. No explicit reuse licence was found on the checked catalogue, product, about, resources or privacy pages. Source attribution and direct product links accompany this collection.
