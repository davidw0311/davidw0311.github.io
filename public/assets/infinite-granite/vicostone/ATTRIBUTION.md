# Vicostone catalogue imagery

Product names, codes, and surface photographs: © their respective owners, Vicostone.
Source: https://vicostone.com/en/product — official global catalogue, audited September 7, 2026.

All 214 unique listed codes are included. Regional availability may vary; this is a design preview, not an inventory or ordering service. Each picker entry links to its supplier product page.

`sources.json` preserves the audited list and original image paths. `catalogue.json` records source URLs, original image dimensions, optimized image checksums, and material metadata. `scripts/generate-vicostone-catalogue.mjs` creates local WebP textures and thumbnails. Rendered pattern scale uses an approximate 126-inch slab width; physical samples and supplier specifications remain authoritative. The renderer samples the central surface area to avoid printed labels at image edges; local photographs retain their original contents.
