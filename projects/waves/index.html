Project story

For PHYS 410 Computational Physics, I studied numerical solutions to the two-dimensional time-dependent Schrödinger equation and modeled wave propagation in three-dimensional space.

I implemented the solver and contour visualizations in MATLAB, then performed convergence testing across barriers, wells, and double-slit experiments.
