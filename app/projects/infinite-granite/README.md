# InfiniteGranite

A browser-only kitchen and bathroom design studio at `/projects/infinite-granite/`, linked from the portfolio's space project deck. No AI calls, uploads, backend, or third-party requests are required while designing. The existing Next.js static export deploys through GitHub Pages.

## Layout and placement

Six presets retain the chosen finishes. A custom plan starts with a 4×4 through 10×10 grid of 36-inch squares. Choose a component and facing, then paint cells on separate floor and wall-cupboard layers. Maximum: 200 components. Starting a new grid is reversible with Undo after building; cancelling keeps the current kitchen.

`cellLayout.ts` owns row/column addresses. Initial pieces fill their cells, so adjacent occupied cells meet without appliance-sized gaps. Width and length edits then enable independent sizing: each piece remains centred on its original cell, while its dimensions change without moving/resizing neighbours, changing grid tracks, or expanding the room. Shrinking can expose space around a piece. Each edge may extend through at most the immediately neighbouring cell, and must stay within the grid and clear of actual component footprints, including range hoods and faucets. Dimensions use half-inch increments, kind-specific minimums, and a 120-inch maximum (further limited by available space). All countertop-height pieces retain a shared 36-inch height; tall pieces retain their appropriate defaults.

The Width and Length controls combine sliders with editable inch fields. Enter or blur applies a typed value, clamped to the displayed available range. Select multiple enables tap/click toggling in the 3D scene, highlights every selected piece, and offers a collapsed checkbox list for keyboard use. A shared dimension applies the same value to every selected piece atomically, including collision checks between selected pieces. Mixed dimensions show a blank field labelled Mixed; an impossible common size disables that dimension. Individual rotate, replace, delete, and movement controls return when multi-select is switched off. Group size edits share one undo action.

View-relative arrows swap with an occupied neighbouring cell on the same layer, or move into an empty one. Rotation changes facing by 90 degrees within the cell. Replacement preserves the cell and component ID, resets component-specific finishes, and changes only that piece to meet the new kind’s minimum dimensions if space permits. Tall-appliance/wall-cupboard conflicts and moves outside the grid are rejected. Deletion leaves the cell empty. Add Component in the stage toolbar switches to a top view and highlights available cells in the 3D scene. Choose a type, then click a green cell; a collapsed cell list offers keyboard placement. Cancel exits placement without adding anything.

Existing saved plans migrate into cells; authored custom-grid addresses are retained and other legacy pieces use the nearest available cells. Unedited presets retain a single back run. Independent dimensions persist through reload, movement, and replacement. Saved grid dimensions and occupancy are validated and canonicalized on restore. `placement.ts` and `connectedEdit.ts` retain legacy geometry utilities and regression coverage. `room.ts` supplies four independently enabled walls, editable heights, wall-bound windows/doors with real openings, frame overlap checks, and backsplash rectangles clipped to actual counter spans and openings. Manual room boundary movement is hidden for cell layouts, whose room and tracks remain fixed during individual resizing.

## Materials and rendering

`materials.ts` provides a supplier-independent catalogue, company grouping, numeric-aware code order, and case/space/punctuation-insensitive code/name search. Eight procedural Studio collection options retain their original IDs. `tceStone.ts` contains all 97 unique products on the five official TCE Stone Canada listing pages audited September 7, 2026. TCE labels them quartz; 21 are marked clearance/discontinued and retain that label. The catalogue heading says 94 colours, but its actual product listings total 97.

TCE photographs are served locally as 97 optimized WebP textures (at most 1536 px / 320 KB each) plus 97 lazy-loaded thumbnails. Full-slab imagery uses published slab dimensions; 20 products have only a detail photo, whose estimated pattern scale is disclosed in the picker. Attribution, source URLs, original image URLs, dimensions and checksums are in `public/assets/infinite-granite/tce/catalogue.json` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`. `node scripts/generate-tce-catalogue.mjs` regenerates typed picker data from the audited manifest. Add another supplier as a separate typed material array, then include it in `MATERIALS`; the picker will group/search it automatically.

Three.js loads only for this route and renders on change. Supplier textures load on demand, with at most three concurrent requests and a bounded texture cache (up to eight entries, or enough for every active material). Loading/failure messages disclose flat-color placeholders. Actual images use an untinted material and world-coordinate mapping. Counters have beveled edges and real sink holes; modeled footprints stay inside module dimensions. Lighting uses environment reflections, daylight shadows, and physical surface finishes. GPU resources are disposed on teardown, eviction, and initialization failure.

Stone previews default to Enhanced pattern visibility, with Natural and Bold options in both finish panels. Luminance contrast strengthens veins and grain while retaining hue, and softer stone reflections reduce washout. Supplier image files remain unchanged; wood bypasses the contrast adjustment. The setting persists with designs and supports undo/redo and legacy saves.

Touch orbit/pinch, keyboard-accessible camera controls, and component dropdowns work on phone and desktop. Fullscreen uses the browser API where available and an edge-to-edge viewport fallback elsewhere, including iPhone. It supports either orientation, resizes the camera, traps focus, restores background scrolling, and offers Exit/Escape. Clicking a kitchen component opens its type selector, Rotate 90°, Delete, four movement arrows, width/length sliders with numeric entry, and Select multiple. Walls and openings retain their separate editors. Add Component is always available in the stage toolbar, including fullscreen. View options contains visibility controls in the reserved editing panel. Show all restores hidden elements without changing the design. Fullscreen includes a collapsible finish panel with the same countertop library, cupboard colours/profiles, and sink/faucet controls as the regular editor. Edit, Room, and View tabs make scene editing and visibility available in fullscreen too. It sits beside the scene on desktop/landscape and below it on portrait phones/tablets, reserving visible space for the kitchen. Changes share persistence and undo/redo with the regular editor; collapsing the panel retains its material search. The viewer follows the visible viewport when the phone keyboard opens. This remains an approximate preview; verify dimensions and physical samples before renovation.

## Persistence and verification

The current design and up to four comparison options persist in `infinitegranite.studio.v1` localStorage. Input validation supports previous saved designs, custom grids, and supplier materials. Storage failure leaves the editor usable. Download saves the current view as a PNG.

Run `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, and `npm run build`. Tests cover preset bounds, placement sweeps/snapping, trapped resizing, keyboard movement, vertical conflicts, room shrinking, a 200-piece grid, repeated save/reload round-trips, all 97 product assets/checksums, catalogue search/order, and actual beveled geometry/sink cutouts at supported size extremes. Browser QA covers custom painting/layers, rejection of overlaps, resizing, persistence, supplier texture selection, and phone portrait/landscape plus desktop fullscreen. Finish-control QA also verifies changing all three categories while expanded, retaining search through changes/collapse, shared undo/redo, returning to the regular editor, reload persistence, and keyboard focus/escape handling.


## Beginner interface

The studio uses one editing panel with three tasks: Finishes, Layout, and Pieces. Normal and fullscreen modes share navigation and content; the background editor is unmounted during fullscreen. Countertop selection starts with search and supplier-grouped swatches, with full catalogues expandable in code order. Edge/backsplash settings, separate cupboard colours, and faucet controls live in labelled expanders. The selected-piece editor contains type replacement, rotation, deletion, movement arrows, two dimensions with sliders/numeric entry, and Select multiple. View options contains visibility and the less frequent camera/export controls. A sticky phone preview remains visible while browsing the editor. Save look and Compare stay labelled on phones.

Manual QA covers choosing a supplier code, matching cupboard colours, replacement and resizing with undo, fullscreen layout selection, shared navigation, and portrait/landscape panel bounds. The appearance uses a consistent light mineral palette for readable swatches and controls.

Cell regression tests cover flush dishwasher joins, occupied swaps, empty moves, intentional gaps, shared dimensions/heights, rotation, replacement, placement availability, upper/tall conflicts, preset migration, invalid saved cells, and repeated edit/save roundtrips. Browser QA verifies direct highlighted-cell placement, replacement, arrows, rotation, both sliders, delete/undo, and portrait/landscape/desktop fullscreen.

Independent-sizing regressions cover unchanged neighbours/tracks/room, atomic multi-selection, actual footprint collisions during resize/add/move, unequal-cell extension limits, rotated footprints, invalid values, and persistence. Browser checks cover numeric entry and clamping, mixed values, grouped undo, scene multi-selection, and portrait/landscape fullscreen bounds.


## Vicostone and bathrooms

The countertop catalogue includes all 214 unique codes on Vicostone’s official global product listing audited September 7, 2026, alongside the 97 TCE Stone and eight Studio designs. `vicostone.ts` is generated by `scripts/generate-vicostone-catalogue.mjs`; original paths, per-product links, attribution, image dimensions, and checksums live in `public/assets/infinite-granite/vicostone/`. The 214 textures and 214 thumbnails total approximately 20 MB, served on demand with the existing bounded texture cache. Each texture stays below 320 KB. The catalogue is global rather than a regional inventory; the picker discloses that availability and pattern scale vary. Vicostone pattern mapping uses a nominal 126-inch width and samples the central image area to avoid printed edge labels, while source images remain intact.

Layout → Bathroom offers Powder room (7.5 × 7.5 ft), Full bathroom (8 × 10.5 ft), and Double vanity (10.5 × 12.5 ft). `bathrooms.ts` defines editable grid-based presets with bathroom vanities, toilets, a glass shower, and a bathtub. Vanity tops use the selected countertop material, ceramic basins and existing sink/faucet controls; cupboard colours and hardware apply to vanities too. Mirrors are part of the vanity component. Bathroom grids permit rooms down to 6 ft while kitchen validation retains its 12 ft minimum. Bathroom fixtures participate in the same replacement, 90-degree rotation, move/swap, independent typed dimensions, multi-selection, collision checks, add/remove, undo, save/reload, and fullscreen flows. Mirror and shower heights prevent invalid upper-cupboard placements. All component types remain available in custom plans.

Verification includes all catalogue images/checksums, global-code completeness, supplier search, all three bathroom presets, collision protection, grouped vanity resizing, replacement/addition, custom bathroom grids, and legacy save compatibility. Browser QA checks full-bath and double-vanity geometry, Vicostone selection, typed fixture edits, fullscreen and mobile bounds, and persistence.
